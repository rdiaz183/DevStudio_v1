## 1. GAMEPLAY ENGINE & CORE RULES

### Deck Constraints
* **Main Deck:** Exactly 50 cards (maximum 4 copies per card title).
* **Mandatory Rank 0 Suite:** Exactly 16 Rank 0 cards (4 playsets) are required in every Main Deck. Every Rank 0 unit features **Dual Utility**:
  1. *Active Utility:* Tap during your turn for resource ramp, draw power, card removal ("pop"), or stat debuffs.
  2. *Defensive Guard:* Discard/Rest from hand or field during either player's turn to block attacks, redirect combat, or mitigate damage.
* **Side Deck:** 10 cards used during Best-of-3 set adjustments.
* **Extra Deck:** 10 cards (Rank-up Commander forms and special summons).

### Resource Engine (Dice Energy)
* Base Energy scales automatically by +1 every turn up to a hard cap of 10â€“12 Energy.
* **First Player Equity:** Player 1 starts with 2 Energy; Player 2 starts with 3 Energy (+1 Energy second-player equity bonus).
* Zero land or resource cards required in deck building.

### Commander Progression & Sortie (Rank 0 â†’ 3)
* Commanders dictate color identity and scale turn-by-turn.
* **Commander Sortie:** Deploying a Rank 3 Commander to the Front Line offers massive combat presence.
* **Defeat Penalty:** If a Rank 3 Commander is defeated in combat, the owner loses **2 Prize Cards** and the Commander enters a 1-turn Overload/Rest state.

### Win Condition & Damage System
* **Prize Deck:** 6 to 8 Prize Cards.
* **Damage Resolution:** Direct combat hits move Prize Cards directly to the defender's hand.

### Field Spatial Limit & Open Items
* **5-Card Field Cap:** Maximum 5 combined unit/item slots on board at any time.
* **No Face-Down Cards:** Items (Equipment, Engines, Instant Elixirs) are placed in open board slots or cast directly from hand.

## 2. COMBAT & PHASE ARCHITECTURE

### Interleaved Action Phase
* **Combined Main & Battle Phases:** Players interleave summoning units, activating item cards, using Rank 0 tap abilities, and declaring individual unit attacks within a single fluid turn structure.

### Persistent ATK Reduction
* Combat damage is persistent across turns ($10\text{k} \text{ vs } 5\text{k}$ combat results in the destruction of the $5\text{k}$ unit while the $10\text{k}$ attacker retains $5\text{k}$ remaining ATK).
* Low-rank units can chip down high-stat boss threats across multiple attacks.

## 3. SEVEN-COLOR ELEMENTAL FRAMEWORK

1. **Red (Fire):** Direct damage, high base ATK, sacrifice offensive plays.
2. **Blue (Water / Ice):** Control, persistent ATK debuffs, board manipulation.
3. **Green (Nature / Earth):** Energy ramp, high-defense walls, stat buffing.
4. **Yellow (Wind / Lightning):** Fast combos, unit re-tapping, multi-attacks.
5. **White (Light / Holy):** Prize card shielding, healing, protection skills.
6. **Purple (Dark / Chaos):** Graveyard recursion, sacrifice disruption.
7. **Grey (Neutral):** Universal utility cards usable by any Commander color identity.

## 5. MATCH CLOCK & ACTION TIMER ENGINE

* **Match Format:** Best-of-3 (BO3) with a **60-minute hard match clock** (25 minutes bank per player, 10-minute side-decking/transition window).
* **Side Decking Window:** 90 seconds between games to swap up to 10 cards.
* **Dynamic Turn Timer:** 90 seconds base turn bank. Playing a card, casting an item, or declaring an attack refunds **+5 seconds** to the active bank (capped at 3 minutes maximum per turn).
* **End Phase Hand Limit:** Enforced **8-card maximum hand size**; excess cards are discarded to the Graveyard.

## 6. BUSINESS, ECONOMY & COMMUNITY MODEL

### Monetization & Player Equity
* **$20 Quarterly Expansion DLC:** Includes 4x playsets of key DLC cards, 4x playsets of all Common set cards, 2 Character Skins, 2 Board Skins, 3D card plastics, and 10,000 In-Game Currency.
* **5-Tier Gacha & 1% Alt-Art Chase:** Standard 5-rarity gacha with duplicate protection. Pulling a 1% Chase Alt-Art permanently unlocks its crafting recipe.
* **3:1 Crafting System:** Convert 3 extra cards of a specific rarity tier into 1 card of the exact same rarity.

### Esports & Profit-Sharing Engine
* **50% Net Revenue Split:** 50% of net platform revenue funds global tournament prize pools ($20,000 Weekly Cash Cups + Monthly Masters Championships).
* **10% Prize Escalator Pool:** 10% of Cosmetic Store sales and 10% of approved community merch sales (such as custom 1,000-step damage counters) directly escalate live tournament prize pools.
* **Grassroots Tournament Engine:** Integrated client bracket system allowing any player or local game store to run Swiss, Single Elimination, or Round Robin tournaments.
* **100% Creator Commercial Rights:** Content creators retain full commercial monetization rights for gameplay, broadcasts, and merchandise.
