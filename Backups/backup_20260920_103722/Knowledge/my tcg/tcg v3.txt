Game System Document: Code510 TCG Engine (v3.0 Final Core)Core Principles & OverviewThis system is built as a controlled open-format digital TCG designed to maximize tactical skill, eliminate resource variance (mana screw/flood), and provide clear comeback mechanics without relying on luck-based triggers.  Format: 1-vs-1 Competitive TCG.  Match Structure: Best 2 out of 3 rounds. Each round targeted at ~20 minutes.  Main Deck Size: 50 cards (up to 4 copies of any card).  Extra Deck Size: 10 cards.  Side Deck Size: 10 cards.  Starting Hand: 7 cards, featuring a mulligan phase at match start.  Hand Size Cap: 7 cards max (enforced during the End Phase; excess discarded to Grave).  Resource & Energy System (Dice Ramp)The game eliminates main-deck resource cards, replacing them with a deterministic, dice-tracked energy pool.  Starting Base: Player 1 starts Turn 1 with 2 Energy. Player 2 starts Turn 1 with 3 Energy (gains +1 bonus energy to balance going second).  Turn Scaling: At the start of every turn, maximum energy increases by +1 (tracked via a single 20-sided die or digital counter) up to a cap of 10â€“12 Energy.  Recovery: All spent energy automatically resets to full at the start of your turn. Commander & Rank MechanicsYour Commander represents your identity on the board.  Color Locking: Your Commander's color dictates the color pie of your deck. Decks can be built mono-color, dual-color, or tri-color depending on archetype cost requirements, supplemented by Grey (Neutral) universal cards.  Rank Progression: Advances automatically during the Commander Rank Phase from Rank 0 $\rightarrow$ Rank 1 $\rightarrow$ Rank 2 $\rightarrow$ Rank 3/4. Higher ranks unlock higher-tier card activations.  Deployment (Sortie): At Rank 3 or 4, a player may deploy their Commander onto the front line as a active combatant.  Overload / Defeat State: If a deployed Commander is destroyed in battle:The defender instantly loses 2 Prize Cards directly to their hand.  The Commander retreats to the Commander Zone in Overload/Rest state.  For 1 full turn cycle, it cannot re-deploy, cannot attack, and all passive/active abilities are completely suppressed.  Card Types & Timing RulesMonsters: Main combat units. Can only be summoned on your turn during the Main Phase.  Items (Equipment & Potions):Equipment: Played on your turn to attach to monsters.  Potions: Played on your turn OR set face-down in the Stash Zone during your Main Phase by paying its cost. Stashed Potions can be triggered instantly on your opponent's turn.  Sorceries: Fast magic spells. Playable on either player's turn as long as you have the required Energy available. Resolves via LIFO (Last-In, First-Out) chain priority.  Combat & Reaction EngineAttacking & Tapping: Attacking requires turning a Front-Line monster sideways (Tapped). Tapping is used exclusively as an indicator of an attack declaration or paying an effect cost.  Damage Resolution & Ties (Mutual Clash):Monsters compare ATK values. Highest ATK destroys the opposing unit.  In the event of equal ATK, Mutual Clash occurs: both combatants are destroyed simultaneously.  Defensive Reactions:Hand Guards: Defenders can discard specific Guard/Shield monster cards directly from their hand during damage calculation to boost defending ATK or absorb damage.  Stashed Potions & Fast Sorceries: Can be cast mid-battle to disrupt attacks or alter combat stats.  Direct Hits & Damage: Direct attacks against an undefended Commander (when Front Line is clear) remove 1 card from the defender's Prize Pool into their hand. Reaching 0 Prize Cards results in game defeat. Turn StructureDraw & Recover Phase: Untap all spent energy, advance Max Energy die by +1, draw 1 card from deck.  Commander Rank Phase: Advance Commander Rank counter by +1.  Main Phase: Play monsters, attach equipment, set Potions into the Stash, cast Sorceries, or deploy Rank 3/4 Commander to the Front Line.  Battle Phase: Declare attacks by tapping units. Resolve Hand Guards, Stash Potions, Fast Sorceries, or Overdrive abilities during the reaction step. Resolve combat outcomes.  End Phase: Check 7-card maximum hand size limit (discard excess to Grave) and pass turn priority.
