Most used megas

In the high-stakes metagame of Pokemon Champions (VGC 2026), Mega Charizard Y, Mega Gengar, and Mega Floette (Eternal) dominate as the top-used Mega Pokemon. These powerhouses are valued for their raw offensive power and Speed, with Mega Charizard Y frequently appearing on top-tier teams in both Tailwind and Trick Room formats.

Top Used Mega Pokemon in Champions (VGC 2026):
Mega Charizard Y: Extremely dominant offensive presence, often centralizing team building around its high sun-boosted damage.
Mega Gengar: Valued for speed and trapping capability, making it a premier threat in the metagame.
Mega Floette (Eternal): Recognized as one of the best Megas, with top players building teams to counter it.
Mega Charizard X: High usage in competitive teams, specifically mentioned for its dominance alongside Y.
Mega Aerodactyl/Mega Froslass: Highly ranked in competitive VGC lists, frequently used in top-8 finishes.

Other Prominent Megas:
Mega Garchomp: Noted for its bulk, though sometimes outclassed by its base form, it remains a common choice.
Mega Lopunny: A top tier threat due to its high speed, with a speed tier of 135 to outspeed Sneasler.
Mega Manectric: While featured, it is generally considered weaker, often placed in lower tiers by analysts
