2. Item "Stashing" & Quick-Potion Reactions
You specified that Sorceries can be played on any turn, but Items (equipment, potions) are limited to your turn. Elevate this with a Stash Slot.
How it works: You can place up to 2 Item cards face-down in a dedicated "Stash" during your Main Phase by paying their cost. Face-down Potions in the Stash can then be activated on your opponent's turn as a Reaction to damage.
The Strategic Hook: It allows items like Healing Potions to serve as mid-combat defensive interrupts, giving high-skill players defensive outplays without introducing complex shields or guard hands.
